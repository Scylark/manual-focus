A working paper from Maximilian Kaiser and Christian Schulze, dated October 2026 and reported on 2 November, gives paid teams the first outside measure of ChatGPT ads. In US traffic a paid ChatGPT visit earned $0.89 against $2.45 for a paid Google Search visit, so a ChatGPT click is worth about $0.26 at search's rate of return.

## Key takeaways

- Kaiser and Schulze found a paid ChatGPT visit generated $0.89 in revenue in US traffic, against $2.45 for a paid Google Search visit, a 64% shortfall.
- Paid ChatGPT visitors converted at 0.37% against 1.17% for paid search, and their orders were $74 smaller after adjustment.
- Of 87 advertisers buying both channels, 73 recorded no paid ChatGPT sale from 18,726 paid ChatGPT sessions.
- The paper puts the parity price of a ChatGPT click at about $0.26, while the largest publicly known account paid $1.72.
- Organic ChatGPT referrals fell by an estimated 6% to 8% after ads launched in a market, and their conversion rate held steady.

## Who measured ChatGPT ads, and how?

Kaiser is at the Vienna University of Economics and Business and Grips Intelligence, and Schulze is at the Frankfurt School of Finance and Management. Their paper, [Can Advertising Fund Free AI?](https://ecommerceresearch.org/chatgpt-ads/), is a working paper under review. Grips Intelligence supplied Google Analytics ecommerce data free of charge, and the authors say it had no role in the research design or writing.

The data covers 60 weeks, from 29 June 2025 to 22 August 2026, and nearly 10 billion sessions on 1,898 online shops that sell $16 billion a year. The authors used OpenAI's staggered rollout as a natural experiment. Ads went live in the US on 9 February 2026, in the UK on 4 June and in the EU on 24 August, after the window closed, so Europe served as the comparison group. The paid analysis uses 387 advertiser websites, of which 102 have full sales tracking and 31 have linked Google Ads cost records.

[Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-chatgpt-ad-revenue-per-visit/595596/) covered the paper on 2 November.

## What a ChatGPT click returned

The headline numbers come from US traffic, where ads ran longest. The [full paper](https://ecommerceresearch.org/chatgpt-ads/Kaiser-Schulze-2026-Can-Advertising-Fund-Free-AI.pdf) compares each channel against direct traffic on the same sites.

| Measure, US traffic | Paid ChatGPT | Paid Google Search |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue per visit | $0.89 | $2.45 |
| Conversion rate | 0.37% | 1.17% |
| Price per click in the study | $1.72, largest public account | $0.72, median of 16 US accounts |
| Price that matches search's return | about $0.26 | $0.72 |
| Gap to direct traffic | Short on all six measures tested | No detectable gap on conversion, order value or revenue |

The authors checked that one big buyer wasn't skewing the result. The largest advertiser carried 66% of paid ChatGPT purchases, and dropping it made the gap wider. Among the 87 advertisers who bought both channels, 97% earned less per ChatGPT visit. Most sites made no sale at all, and 14 websites carried all 570 paid ChatGPT transactions.

The paper also compared each advertiser's sales in the channels they didn't buy, before and after their first paid ChatGPT week, and found no detectable lift there. That cuts against the claim that ChatGPT ads pay back somewhere the last click can't see, though the authors say their test rules out only large indirect effects.

## What does this mean for a paid budget?

For a performance team, ChatGPT ads are a test channel that has to earn a bid. If your paid search clicks cost £0.90 and return £3, the paper's ratio suggests a ChatGPT click returns about £1.09, so paying more than about £0.33 loses money against search on the tracked sale. The paper cites OpenAI's recommended starting bid as $3 to $5 per click. Your own ratio will differ, so work it out before the next budget meeting.

OpenAI added a scale figure this week. Its ChatGPT consumer product lead Adam Fry told a New York dinner, as [Big Technology reported on 3 November](https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/openai-chatgpt-search-is-about-20), that ChatGPT's volume of search-like queries is roughly one fifth of Google's, and that weekly users rose from 1 billion to 1.2 billion in two months. Audience size makes ChatGPT hard to ignore, but it doesn't change what each click returns, so it shouldn't set the bid.

Our view at Manual Focus is that UK advertisers should cap ChatGPT bids at their own parity price and hold spend at test level until a holdout on their own account shows incremental sales, because the first outside data shows ChatGPT clicks returning about a third of a search click. We said the same on [OpenAI's measurement update in October](/blog/chatgpt-ads-measurement-visual-ads/), and this paper is the first outside evidence for it.

Brand and SEO teams have a smaller signal to watch. Organic ChatGPT referrals fell by an estimated 6% to 8% once ads arrived, and visitors who still came converted at 2.21% before and almost exactly the same after. Organic ChatGPT already converts about as well as organic search, according to the paper, so being cited in the answer remains worth more than paying for the card beneath it.

## Four moves before Black Friday

1. **Calculate your parity price.** Take your median paid search cost per click and multiply it by your ChatGPT revenue per visit divided by your paid search revenue per visit. Set ChatGPT bid caps at or below that figure.
2. **Split ChatGPT out of blended reports.** Make paid ChatGPT its own channel in GA4 and in any attribution tool, so a small number of sessions can't hide inside a healthy total. The [attribution tear-down playbook](/lens/demand/attribution-teardown/) sets out how.
3. **Run a holdout before scaling.** Pause ChatGPT ads in a set of regions for four weeks and compare total sales against regions that kept running. The paper found no lift in channels advertisers didn't buy, so a short pause should cost little. Agree the threshold with finance first.
4. **Check the creative setting.** OpenAI switched on [creative enhancements by default](/blog/chatgpt-ads-creative-enhancements-default/) at the end of October, so a weak test may also be a rewritten ad you never approved.

If you sell through Shopify, our post on [ChatGPT Ads for UK ecommerce](/blog/chatgpt-ads-shopify-uk-ecommerce/) covers the setup, and the [paid search bidding agent playbook](/lens/demand/paid-search-bidding-agent/) shows how to run margin-aware bid caps across channels.

## Where the study runs out

The data stops on 22 August 2026, before OpenAI added ten attribution partners in October, before the visual ad test and before creative enhancements became the default, so performance may have changed since. The paper counts the last click only and can't see prompts, impressions or which tier a user was on, and ads appear only on the free and Go tiers. Advertisers chose to buy ChatGPT ads, so the results describe who bought rather than proving cause. The UK had under three months of ads in the window, and the headline figures are from US traffic. The $1.72 price comes from one public account, and the parity price assumes similar margins in both channels. The paper is also still under review, and peer review may change the numbers.
