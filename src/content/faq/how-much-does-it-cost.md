It depends on the shape of the work. Strategic Advisor is a day rate or a monthly retainer for one or two days a month. Fractional CMO is one or two days a week on a monthly fee. Build & Hand Over is a fixed price for a fixed scope, with typical builds taking 4 to 12 weeks. Manual Focus AI is a monthly retainer. The first 30-minute working session is free, and you get a written scope with the cost before anything starts.
