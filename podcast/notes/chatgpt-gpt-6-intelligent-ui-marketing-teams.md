James and Hannah talk through GPT-6 and Intelligent UI, which OpenAI began
rolling out in ChatGPT on 7 October 2026. Answers can now arrive as
side-by-side comparisons, maps, forms and small tools built on request,
including a savings calculator. They cover who gets which model and when, why
a generic calculator or comparison table is no longer a defensible traffic
asset, what Radisson's plugin result does and doesn't prove, what OpenAI
hasn't said yet, and three jobs to hand to AI.

## Get AI on this

Paste these into whichever AI assistant you use: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or
Copilot. Swap the bracketed parts for your own details. The first needs file
uploads and the ability to build a simple interactive tool or code; the
second needs an assistant that can browse the web; the third needs a file
upload and works best set to run once, after 5 November.

### 1. Audit your interactive tools

```text
I run marketing for [brand], [one line on what you sell] at [website], selling
mainly in [market].

ChatGPT can now build small tools inside a conversation on request, such as a
calculator for how savings could grow. I want to know which of our website
tools a buyer could now get in the chat instead.

Attached is an analytics export listing every calculator, quiz, guide and
comparison table on our site, with sessions and leads for each over the last
[period].

For each tool:
1. Build your own version of it from a one-line request, as a buyer would
   ask for it (for example "build me a calculator for how much I'd save over
   five years").
2. Compare your version with ours, using the URL in the export.
3. Say whether ours depends on anything you can't make up: live prices,
   stock, a customer's own account data, or a quote we will honour.

Give me a table with the columns: Tool | URL | Sessions | Leads | Could you
match it? (yes/partly/no) | What ours has that yours doesn't | What ours relies
on that only we can supply (or "nothing").

Sort by leads, highest first. Don't recommend what we should add; that's our
call. If you couldn't open a tool or build a fair version of it, say so in
that row rather than guessing.
```

### 2. Put comparison facts in plain text

```text
We sell [product category] at [website] and compete with [named rivals].

When an AI assistant compares products in columns, it fills each cell with
whatever it can find about price, specs and delivery. I want our facts to be
easy to read on every product page.

Here are our product page URLs: [paste list].

For each page, check whether these are stated in plain, crawlable text (not
only in an image, PDF or download):
- price
- key specs: [list the specs that matter in our category]
- dimensions
- delivery terms

Give me:
1. A table with the columns: URL | Price in text? | Key specs in text? |
   Dimensions in text? | Delivery terms in text? | Where the missing facts
   currently live (image, PDF, download, not found) | Format matches other
   pages? (yes/no).
2. For each product, a draft fact block in one consistent format, using only
   facts you found on our pages. Leave a [blank] wherever a fact is missing
   rather than filling it in.

List any page you couldn't load or read in full.
```

### 3. Measure ChatGPT referrals before and after GPT-6

```text
Run this once, after 5 November 2026.

GPT-6 reached free ChatGPT users from 8 October 2026. I want to see whether
ChatGPT referrals to our site, and conversions on our tool pages, changed
after that date.

Attached is an analytics export covering 10 September to 5 November 2026, with
daily sessions and conversions by referral source and landing page. Our tool
pages are: [paste URLs of calculators, comparison tables and other tools].

Compare the four weeks before 8 October with the four weeks after it.

Give me:
1. A table with the columns: Measure | Four weeks before | Four weeks after |
   Change | Change (%), with rows for ChatGPT referral sessions, ChatGPT
   referral conversions, and sessions and conversions for each tool page.
2. Three to five sentences on what moved most.

Only report what the data shows; don't explain why something changed. If
ChatGPT referrals can't be separated from other sources in the export, or a
date range is incomplete, say so before giving any numbers.
```

### And one for you

Re-run your 20 most important buyer questions on a Free ChatGPT account (GPT-6
Luna) and a Plus account (GPT-6 Sol) once GPT-6 reaches them. Screenshot the
format of each answer and note whether you appear in any comparison. It needs
two sign-ins and your own judgement on what the answers mean for you.
