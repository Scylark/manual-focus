OpenAI said its ChatGPT Ads app for Shopify would start opening to merchants outside the US on 23 September 2026, the day ChatGPT Ads passed 60 countries. Because the UK already has self-serve buying, a UK Shopify brand can test ads in ChatGPT without an agency, reaching people on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans.

## Key takeaways

- OpenAI launched the ChatGPT Ads app for US Shopify merchants on 16 September 2026, naming Shopify its first ecommerce partner and HubSpot its first CRM partner.
- On 23 September 2026 OpenAI took ChatGPT Ads into seven more Asian markets, which put it in more than 60 countries.
- OpenAI's Ads Manager lists the United Kingdom for self-service, so a business based in the UK can buy ChatGPT ads without an agency.
- OpenAI shows ChatGPT ads only to people on the Free and Go plans and says advertising does not influence ChatGPT's answers.
- In November 2026 OpenAI's help centre still described the Shopify ads integration as US-only, with other ChatGPT Ads markets to follow "shortly".

**Update, 11 November 2026.** OpenAI's own help centre says the Shopify app hasn't reached UK merchants yet. Its [setup guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001523-set-up-chatgpt-ads-for-shopify), last updated in October, says the integration is "currently available to all Shopify customers in the US" and will reach other ChatGPT Ads markets "shortly". On 10 November OpenAI launched [Businesses in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) in beta, where a Shopify merchant can connect its store to claim a business profile, though Business Agents are US-only for now. The opt-in text setting described below has since grown into [creative enhancements](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001614-creative-enhancements-for-chatgpt-ads), on by default for self-serve campaigns since 30 October, as [our post on automatic rewrites](/blog/chatgpt-ads-creative-enhancements-default/) explains. The first outside study of returns found a paid ChatGPT visit earned $0.89 against $2.45 for paid Google Search in US traffic, as set out in [our post on that study](/blog/chatgpt-ads-returns-vs-google-search-study/).

## What OpenAI launched in September

The Shopify app arrived in a larger [announcement on 16 September](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/). OpenAI named Shopify its first ecommerce partner and HubSpot its first CRM partner. Because products are "already integrated through Shopify Catalog", a merchant can start running ads and tracking performance straight away. US merchants got the app that day. According to [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/shopify-becomes-openais-first-e-commerce-partner-with-chatgpt-ads-integration/), OpenAI planned to open it to other ChatGPT Ads markets from 23 September.

The same announcement added AI tools on the advertiser side. Advertisers can now build and analyse campaigns by prompting ChatGPT through an Ads Manager plugin, while Ads Manager itself suggests copy and imagery from the landing page and the campaign objective. OpenAI also launched an opt-in setting that rewrites an advertiser's headlines and descriptions to fit each conversation and translates them into the user's language. Sponsored Agents, which let a shopper who clicks an ad carry on the conversation with an agent the brand sponsors, went into a test with select US advertisers.

A week later, on 23 September, OpenAI [took ChatGPT Ads into seven more Asian markets](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-southeast-asia-taiwan/) and put the total at more than 60 countries. It said the business reached $1 billion in annualised revenue in less than 200 days and that tens of thousands of advertisers now run ads in ChatGPT. Its [availability page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability) lists the United Kingdom for self-service. [Digiday reported](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-ads-business-hits-europe-at-the-six-month-mark/) in August that UK advertising started in June through agency representatives, so British advertisers went from needing an agency to buying self-serve in about three months.

## How can a UK brand buy ChatGPT ads?

A UK business has four routes into ChatGPT advertising, of which two are fully open today.

| Route | UK status in November | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Ads Manager self-serve | Open | A first test on your own budget |
| OpenAI Ads team or agency partner | Open since June | Larger advertisers |
| ChatGPT Ads app for Shopify | US only, others "shortly" | Shopify brands once it arrives |
| Business Hub and Business Agents | Hub in beta, agents US only | Claiming your profile in ChatGPT |

The Shopify app saves setup work once it arrives. OpenAI's setup guide says it syncs your catalogue and commerce events into Ads Manager through the OpenAI Pixel for Shopify, so product ads and conversion tracking need less manual setup.

## Who sees ads in ChatGPT?

OpenAI shows ads only to people on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans, so every paid subscription stays ad-free. Ads won't reach senior buyers who use a paid ChatGPT plan at work, so for B2B sellers the only way into those conversations is still being the source ChatGPT cites. We covered how far citations have drifted from Google rankings in [The citation moved off the page](/blog/the-citation-moved-off-the-page/).

OpenAI also says ads don't influence ChatGPT's answers. Buying ads won't get your products recommended in the answer itself, so you have to run paid and organic as separate efforts, even though both appear on the same screen. Organic answers carry their own lean toward big retailers, which [Lightspeed's AI shopping study](/blog/ai-shopping-assistants-favour-big-retailers/) measured a week later.

## What changes for UK ecommerce teams

The ads draw on whatever sits in your Shopify catalogue, so product titles and images do the job ad creative used to do. A thin or inconsistent catalogue now costs you in a paid channel as well as in organic AI answers.

Our view at Manual Focus is that a UK Shopify brand with clear product-level demand should run a small, controlled test this autumn, paid for from its experimental budget rather than cut from paid search or social that already performs. We'd keep OpenAI's automatic rewriting off at first, because it rewrites your headlines in your name and you should see what it produces before you let it run. Since 30 October that means opting out in Ads Manager. Paid channels are turning into contests between AI systems on both sides, which we wrote about in [Paid channels in the AI era](/blog/paid-channels-bidding-agents/).

## How we'd run a first test

1. **Tidy the catalogue first.** Fix the product copy and images in the categories you plan to advertise, because the ads draw on them.
2. **Start with one category.** Choose one where shoppers ask detailed questions before they buy, such as sizing or compatibility, since those are the conversations ChatGPT handles.
3. **Test the automatic copy separately.** Run a separate campaign with creative enhancements on, since the setting works at account and campaign level, then compare what it writes and converts against your own copy.
4. **Measure against paid search.** Tag every click, then compare cost per order and returning-customer rate with your Google Shopping campaigns for the same products. The first outside study found paid ChatGPT visits returned about a third of a paid search visit in US traffic, so [set a bid cap](/blog/chatgpt-ads-returns-vs-google-search-study/) before you start. The [attribution tear-down](/lens/demand/attribution-teardown/) playbook shows how to separate genuine lift from double counting.
5. **Keep working on organic citations.** Nobody on a paid ChatGPT plan sees the ads, which leaves citations as your only way into those conversations.

## What's still unclear

OpenAI said the Shopify app would reach other markets from 23 September without naming them, so merchants should check the App Store listing from their own account. The first outside study reports its headline figures from US traffic, so there's no clear UK performance figure yet. OpenAI has given no date for Sponsored Agents outside the US and says it will build new formats and measurement tools over the next few months.
