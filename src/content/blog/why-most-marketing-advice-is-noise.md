Most marketing advice is noise because it was written for a search engine that ranked pages, while buyers now ask AI assistants that quote a few sources. In March 2026, G2 found that 51% of B2B software buyers start research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google. Advice that ignores that shift is out of date on arrival.

## Key takeaways

- Google users clicked a traditional search result in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared, against 15% of visits without one, in Pew Research browsing data from 900 US adults in March 2025.
- Only 12% of the links cited by AI assistants such as ChatGPT ranked in Google's top 10 for the same prompt, in an Ahrefs study of 15,000 prompts published in August 2025.
- Half of B2B software buyers (51%) started research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google in G2's March 2026 survey, up from 29% a year earlier.
- A strategy written before LLM-based search needs rebuilding around how AI assistants find and cite brands, with planning cycles measured in weeks.

## Why is most marketing advice outdated?

Every week brings a new ultimate guide to SEO or a new framework promising 10x growth. Most of it was outdated when it was published. AI now shapes how buyers discover brands and decide what to buy in real time, so the advice lags reality by a wide margin.

## How has AI changed discovery?

Traditional SEO is losing ground to LLM-powered answer engines. When Google shows an AI summary, [Pew Research found](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) that users click a traditional result in 8% of visits, against 15% when there's no summary. Content strategies built for 2020 no longer capture buyer attention the way they used to.

Buyers in B2B and B2C alike now use AI assistants to research and shortlist. [G2's March 2026 survey](https://company.g2.com/news/g2-research-the-answer-economy) found that 51% of B2B software buyers start with an AI chatbot more often than with Google, up from 29% a year earlier. Those assistants skip past your brand guidelines and landing page copy to look for clear, checkable answers.

If your strategy predates LLM-based search, it needs a full rethink. For the channel-by-channel breakdown, start with how AI is rewiring [owned channels](/blog/owned-channels-website-to-platform/). The posts on [earned channels](/blog/earned-channels-pr-in-the-ai-era/) and [paid channels](/blog/paid-channels-bidding-agents/) follow the same logic.

## What matters in marketing now?

**Answer engine optimisation.** Structure your content so AI systems can parse and cite it. Keywords alone won't get you there, because an AI answer quotes a handful of sources. In an [Ahrefs study of 15,000 prompts](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-overlap/), only 12% of the links cited by AI assistants such as ChatGPT ranked in Google's top 10 for the same prompt, which is why [Google rankings no longer predict who AI answers cite](/blog/the-citation-moved-off-the-page/). The [SEO playbook for premium endurance brands](/blog/seo-playbook-premium-endurance-brands/) covers the technical and content moves that make this work.

**AI-native distribution.** The channels have shifted, so you need to understand how LLMs choose which sources to trust and what credibility looks like when there's no click. [Light Meter](/blog/manual-focus-light-meter-ai-visibility/) is how Manual Focus measures which brands AI engines recommend to UK buyers.

**Speed over perfection.** AI can compress parts of the marketing cycle from months to days. Companies that adapt quickly gain an edge over those locked in six-month planning cycles. [The 90-day AI integration plan](/blog/the-90-day-ai-integration-plan/) is what that adaptation looks like for a marketing leader.

Each old habit has a replacement.

| Old advice | What matters now |
|---|---|
| Rank in Google's top three | Be the source an AI answer quotes |
| Win the click | Win the mention when there's no click |
| Fill the content calendar | Answer the buyer's question in the first paragraph |
| Polish the landing page copy | Give AI assistants clear facts they can check |
| Plan in six-month cycles | Test and adjust in weeks |

Our view at Manual Focus is that a marketing plan written before LLM-based search needs rebuilding, because the way buyers find brands has changed underneath it.
