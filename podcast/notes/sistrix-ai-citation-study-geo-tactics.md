James and Hannah go through the SISTRIX study published on 22 October 2026,
which compared 96,418 pages to see which GEO tactics go with citations in
ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews. Tables of contents, Article schema,
real tables and author boxes went with more citations, llms.txt showed no
measurable effect, and product pages did poorly. They cover how to read the
title-year finding, what it means for online shops, why small GEO studies
mislead, and three jobs to hand to AI.

## Get AI on this

Paste these into whichever AI assistant you use. Swap the bracketed parts for
your own details. The first needs an assistant that can open web pages, the
second and third need file uploads.

### 1. Score your advice pages

```text
I run marketing for [brand], [one line on what you sell] at [website], selling
mainly in [market].

A SISTRIX study of 96,418 URLs, published in October 2026, found these page
features went with more citations in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews:
a table of contents, five or more H2 headings, at least one H2 phrased as a
question, a table with a header row, an author box with a photo, a visible
last-updated date, Article schema and FAQPage schema used for genuine
questions.

Here are our 20 most important advice and guide pages: [paste URLs].

Open each page and its source code and check each feature. Give me a table
with the columns: URL | Table of contents | 5+ H2s | Question H2 | Table with
header row | Author box with photo | Visible update date | Article schema |
FAQPage schema | Features missing (count).

Then rank the pages by features missing, most first, and for the top five list
the specific fixes. If you couldn't open a page or read its schema, say so in
that row rather than guessing.
```

### 2. Match rival citations to your pages

```text
I run marketing for [brand] at [website].

Attached is an export of Top Opportunity Queries from the Topics Portfolio
report in Bing Webmaster Tools. These are AI answer queries where other sites
are cited and ours isn't. Also attached is a list of our advice and guide
pages with their titles: [attach or paste].

For each query, find the page on our site that should answer it. Give me a
table with the columns: Query | Topic | Our best matching page (or "none") |
How well it answers the query (fully, partly, not at all) | What the page is
missing.

Group the queries with no matching page into suggested new pages, each with a
working title and the queries it would cover. Don't invent search volumes or
citation counts that aren't in the export.
```

### 3. Plan an advice section for a shop

```text
I run marketing for [brand], an online shop selling [product categories] in
[market].

A SISTRIX study published in October 2026 found that advice and how-to pages
made up 48.7% of pages cited by AI search, while product pages were cited
less often. I want to plan an advice section.

Attached are our product categories and the questions customers ask us most,
from [help-desk tags, reviews or site search, as available].

Propose 10 to 15 guide pages. For each give: a working title phrased as the
customer's question, the products it would link to, an outline of five or
more H2 sections, one comparison table it should include, and who on our team
is best placed to be named as the author.

Base every topic on the questions in the attachments, and say which
attachment each one came from. Flag any topic where you're unsure customers
actually ask it.
```
