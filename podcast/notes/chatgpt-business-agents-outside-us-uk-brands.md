James and Hannah talk through OpenAI's 19 November 2026 update, which opened
ChatGPT Business Agents to ecommerce and online service businesses outside the
US, and the Sponsored Business Agents now rolling out in Ads Manager. They
cover who can build an agent and what verification it takes, what an agent can
and can't do for a customer, how a sponsored agent changes the ad click and
its reporting, and why a UK brand should test an agent unpaid before it
spends money sending shoppers there.

## Get AI on this

Paste these into whichever AI assistant you use. Swap the bracketed parts for
your own details. Both prompts need file uploads or pasted page text.

### 1. Draft the agent's brief from your own pages

```text
I run marketing for [brand], [one line on what you sell] at [website], selling
mainly in [market].

We are setting up a Business Agent in ChatGPT, a chat that represents our
business, explains our products and policies and sends customers to our
website. It cannot complete purchases, make bookings or check orders. I need a
draft set of instructions for the agent, written only from our own published
pages.

Attached (or pasted below) are the current text of:
- our main product or service pages
- our delivery page
- our returns, refunds and warranty page
- any FAQ page

Write the agent instructions as:
1. A short description of the business and who it serves.
2. What the agent may say about products, delivery, returns and warranty, as
   plain statements.
3. What the agent must not say or promise, including anything about orders,
   bookings or payments.
4. When the agent should send the customer to a named page on our site.

After each statement in sections 2 and 4, put the source page in brackets.
Then give a separate table with the columns: Topic | What page A says | What
page B says | Conflict to resolve, for every place where our pages disagree.

Use only what the pages say. Don't invent policies, prices or timings. If a
topic a customer would ask about isn't covered by any page, list it under
"Not covered" instead of filling the gap.
```

### 2. Test the agent's answers before you publish

```text
I run marketing for [brand] at [website]. We have built a Business Agent in
ChatGPT and previewed it before publishing. I need to check its answers
against our website.

Attached are:
- the 20 questions our customer service team receives most, with how often
  each comes in
- the agent's answer to each question, copied from the preview
- the current text of our product, delivery, returns and FAQ pages

For each question, compare the agent's answer with what our pages say. Give
me a table with the columns: Question | Times asked | Agent's answer (short) |
What our site says | Verdict (right, wrong or unclear) | Suggested fix to the
agent instructions.

Sort the table so wrong answers come first, then unclear, then right, and
within each group by how often the question is asked. Quote the page text you
relied on. If our pages don't answer a question, mark the verdict "site
doesn't say" rather than judging the agent. Don't assume any policy that isn't
in the attached text.

We'll re-run this check whenever our delivery or returns terms change.
```
