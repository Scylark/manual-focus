OpenAI began rolling out GPT-6 with Intelligent UI on 7 October 2026 to the more than 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT each week. The model can now compose an answer as an interactive interface, such as a side-by-side comparison or a small tool built on request, so the page where buyers meet your brand has changed shape.

## Key takeaways

- OpenAI started rolling out GPT-6 with Intelligent UI in ChatGPT's Chat tab on 7 October 2026 for paid plans, expanding to Free and Go users from 8 October.
- Paid ChatGPT plans run GPT-6 Sol in the Chat tab, while the Free and Go plans run GPT-6 Luna.
- Intelligent UI lets ChatGPT build an answer as an interactive interface and create small tools on request, such as a calculator for how savings could grow.
- For questions that need web search, GPT-6 Instant starts answering 44% sooner on average than GPT-5.6 Instant, by OpenAI's own measure.
- Radisson's ChatGPT plugin converted visits to bookings at about 1.5 times Radisson's organic search rate in July and August 2026, according to OpenAI's case study.

**Update, 11 November 2026.** OpenAI has now said what brands can feed into ChatGPT's answers. Its [Businesses in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) beta, launched on 10 November, lets an eligible business claim a profile in ChatGPT and keep its details current. Retailers can upload product feeds with pricing and availability, which [OpenAI says](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/) ChatGPT may use to answer customer questions. That gives a brand its own way to supply the facts behind the comparison cells described below, though OpenAI says claiming "does not guarantee higher placement or sales". Business Hub also reports mentions and website clicks, which gives you a second referral baseline beside your analytics. Separately, an October 2026 study estimated that organic ChatGPT referrals fell 6% to 8% after ads launched in a market, as [our post on the returns study](/blog/chatgpt-ads-returns-vs-google-search-study/) explains.

## How Intelligent UI works

OpenAI's [announcement](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/) says GPT-6 was trained to compose responses from text and interactive elements, choosing the mix for each question. A comparison can appear side by side, while the stops on a road trip can sit on a map with notes on which ones are worth a detour. Users can also ask for a tool, such as a calculator for how savings could grow or a bill splitter. OpenAI built a library of native components and a compiler that renders the interface as the model writes it, so the layout appears progressively.

Speed changed too, because ChatGPT can now begin answering while it is still thinking. For questions that need web search, GPT-6 Instant starts answering 44% sooner on average than GPT-5.6 Instant. OpenAI says GPT-6 also makes better decisions about when to look something up.

### Which ChatGPT plans get GPT-6?

According to the [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes), the rollout split by plan.

| ChatGPT plan | Model in the Chat tab | Rollout began |
|---|---|---|
| Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise | GPT-6 Sol | 7 October 2026 |
| Free and Go | GPT-6 Luna | 8 October 2026 |
| Pro reasoning option | GPT-6 Astra, without Intelligent UI | Unchanged |

Enterprise availability depends on admin settings, while the models behind Work and Codex didn't change.

## What happens to brand calculators?

Many brands built traffic and lead capture on simple interactive tools, such as savings calculators and product comparison tables. A buyer who can type "build me a calculator for how much I'd save over five years" and get a working one in the chat has less reason to click through to yours. OpenAI chose a savings calculator as a launch example, which should get the attention of every finance brand that ranks for "savings calculator".

## What goes into a ChatGPT comparison?

Brands that sell against named rivals face a different risk from the comparison layout. When ChatGPT puts three products in columns, it fills each cell with whatever it found about price and specs. A brand whose facts are scattered across PDFs and inconsistent product pages risks a blank or wrong cell next to a competitor whose numbers were easy to read. OpenAI didn't say at launch what data the comparisons draw on, so that is our inference from how ChatGPT search already works.

Content teams have a smaller adjustment to make. An answer that starts while ChatGPT is still searching rewards pages that state the key fact early in plain language, which is the same discipline our [SEO cluster generator](/lens/content/seo-cluster-generator/) playbook builds into briefs.

## What to rebuild on your site

Our view at Manual Focus is that a generic calculator or comparison table is no longer a defensible traffic asset, so brands should rebuild those tools around what ChatGPT can't make up, such as live prices or a quote you will honour.

| Tool on your site | What ChatGPT can't make up |
|---|---|
| Savings or mortgage calculator | Your live rates and a quote you'll honour |
| Product comparison table | Current prices and stock |
| Size and fit guide | Fit data from your own customers |
| ROI estimator | Figures from the buyer's own account |

That value can live on your site or inside a ChatGPT plugin. Radisson's [ChatGPT plugin](https://openai.com/index/radisson/), which shows hotels on a map with prices and amenities, converted visits to bookings at about 1.5 times Radisson's organic search rate in July and August 2026, according to OpenAI's case study. OpenAI picked that result to publish, so treat it as a signal. Our [post on dots and ChatGPT plugins](/blog/openai-dots-chatgpt-plugins-marketing-teams/) covers how to scope one.

## What to do this autumn

1. **Re-run your category prompts on both tiers.** Once GPT-6 reaches your account, test your 20 most important buyer questions on a Free account (Luna) and a Plus account (Sol). Screenshot the format of each answer and note whether you appear in any comparison.
2. **Audit your interactive tools.** List every calculator and comparison table on your site with its traffic and leads, then ask ChatGPT to build the same thing. Where its version is as good as yours, plan what you will add that only you can supply. Our [site audit and refresh playbook](/lens/ops/site-audit-and-refresh/) covers the wider review.
3. **Put comparison facts in plain text.** Make price and key specs readable on each product page in the same format, rather than in images or downloads.
4. **Set a new referral baseline.** Mark 8 October in your analytics now, then after 5 November compare ChatGPT referral sessions and tool-page conversions for the four weeks either side.
5. **Test your plugin, if you have one.** Check whether ChatGPT still surfaces it for the prompts it should win now that answers can carry their own maps and forms.

## Gaps in what OpenAI published

OpenAI hasn't said whether Intelligent UI changes how ChatGPT links out to websites, or whether ads can appear inside interactive answers. Nor has it explained how a plugin and a model-built interface compete for the same question. It gave no traffic or engagement figures and says there is "still work ahead" on the model's design judgment, so the formats will keep changing. OpenAI measured the 44% figure itself and based its quality claims on internal evaluations, which no outside group has checked.
