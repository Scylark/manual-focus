James and Hannah talk through SparkToro's study of 118,163 ChatGPT prompts
from 12,321 US users, published on 17 November 2026. Only 6.9% of prompts
showed research into a brand to buy from, against more than 15% of Google
queries in SparkToro's earlier work, while 12.1% of ChatGPT responses named a
brand, product or company. They cover how the Verso panel was built and
labelled, why most brands should weight AI visibility budgets towards Google's
AI Overviews and AI Mode, the two exceptions, why Manual Focus Light Meter's
indoor cycling study says each engine needs measuring on its own and more
than once, and three jobs to hand to AI.

## Get AI on this

Paste these into whichever AI assistant you use. Swap the bracketed parts for
your own details. The first and third need file uploads, and the second needs
an assistant that can run on a schedule if you want it monthly.

### 1. Rebuild the ChatGPT prompt set

```text
I run marketing for [brand], [one line on what you sell], selling mainly in
[market].

We track a set of prompts in ChatGPT to see whether it names our brand. A
SparkToro study of US ChatGPT users (published 17 November 2026) found that
only 6.9% of prompts involved researching a brand to buy from. The largest
use categories were images and visual design, information and entertainment,
everyday life and admin, writing, health and wellness, and money, work and
business.

Attached is our current list of tracked prompts.

1. Label each prompt as purchase research ("best X", "X vs Y") or another
   intent.
2. Pick the one or two large use categories above that sit closest to our
   category, and explain the choice in two sentences.
3. Write 15 new advice-led prompts in those categories that a real person
   might type, where a brand like ours could plausibly come up in the answer
   without being asked for.
4. Propose a final set of 25 prompts, keeping the strongest purchase-research
   prompts and adding the best advice-led ones.

Give the final set as a table with the columns: Prompt | Intent | Use
category | Kept or new | Did the answer name a brand the user didn't ask
about? (leave blank for us to fill in).

Don't invent search volumes or claim how often any prompt is asked. If you
can't judge how close a category is to ours, say so.
```

### 2. Put ChatGPT next to Google in one report

```text
Set this up as a routine that runs on the first working day of each month.

Attached is our analytics export of sessions and conversions by source and
medium for the last three months, for [website].

Build one table with a row per month and the columns: Google organic
sessions | Google paid sessions | ChatGPT referral sessions | Other AI
assistant referral sessions | Conversions for each of those | Each source's
share of total sessions and of total conversions.

Identify ChatGPT and other AI assistant referrals from the referrer or source
names in the file, and list exactly which source names you counted for each
row so we can check them.

Under the table, write three sentences on how ChatGPT's share changed against
the previous month. If a source name is ambiguous, or a month is missing from
the file, say so rather than guessing.
```

### 3. Propose a tracking budget split by engine

```text
We are deciding how to split our AI visibility work between engines for
[brand] in [market].

Attached is a list of what we spend on AI visibility today: tools, agency or
freelance costs and team hours per month, with the engine each one covers
(Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, others).

Context. A SparkToro study published on 17 November 2026 found 6.9% of
ChatGPT prompts involved researching a brand to buy from, against more than
15% of Google queries in an earlier SparkToro study, and its author estimates
Google sees purchase-journey queries at 16 times or more the rate of
ChatGPT. The study is US only and its panel skews younger and mobile.

1. Summarise our current split by engine as a share of cost and of hours.
2. Propose a new split weighted towards Google's AI answers, with ChatGPT
   kept as a smaller tracked channel.
3. Write out every assumption behind the new split as a numbered list, so we
   can challenge each one.

Give the current and proposed split as one table with the columns: Engine |
Current cost | Current hours | Proposed cost | Proposed hours | Reason.

This is a proposal for a person to decide on. Don't present it as a
forecast, and flag any line in our spend you couldn't assign to an engine.
```
