An overview of UK gravel

What UK riders search for in gravel, across 43 brands, 77 models, kit and events, and what it means for the next wave of riders.

14 October 2026UK focus, global context2021 to 2026Measures search interest, not sales

01Summary

i

What this report measures. This is a study of UK Google search interest, built from about 6,900 search terms, each searched at least 20 times a month in at least one year. Totals are estimates and are rounded where they lead. The shares, rankings and changes are the most reliable parts. Search shows what riders are considering, comparing and planning. It is not sales data, so use it to compare brands, models and trends over time, not to size the market.

UK search interest in gravel bikes grew to 2023/24 and has held broadly level since, while searches for road bikes have fallen. Gravel's share of bike searches keeps rising as a result. Industry sales reports point to gravel sales still growing, and section 02 explains why search and sales can tell different stories. Searches for gravel events are still rising, and the people searching are a broader group than road racing has traditionally reached.

Five findings

  1. Gravel holds a growing share of bike searches. Gravel now accounts for almost three in ten UK searches for bikes by brand and model, up from 27% in 2022/23.
  2. Search interest has levelled off. Searches for gravel bikes by brand and model rose 8% in 2023/24 and have eased slightly since. That is not the same as sales stalling. A familiar category needs fewer searches per buyer.
  3. Gravel searches are holding while road searches fall. Since 2023/24, road searches by brand and model have fallen by about 420,000 a year and gravel searches by about 60,000. That gap is where gravel's rising share of searches comes from.
  4. Event searches keep rising. Searches for named gravel events rose by almost half in three years, and the biggest UK events sell out.
  5. The people searching are a broader group. Women's search interest in gravel racing is close to men's, and well ahead of their interest in road racing. The barriers that stop new riders, traffic above all, are the ones gravel routes avoid.
!

The search data points to the next opportunity being newer riders, not more bikes for existing cyclists. They need a clear, low-pressure way in through routes, events, kit advice and shops that speak to them.

02Brand and model interest

The last public sales figures give the context. The UK bike market is at its lowest level in about fifty years, at roughly 1.45 million bikes in 2024. Gravel went against that trend. Around 30,000 gravel bikes sold in the year to July 2024, up 15% on the year before and nearly four times the pre-pandemic level, according to Bicycle Association figures reported by Cycling Weekly. It grew 8% in 2022 while the overall market fell 22%.

The same pattern shows in Europe. Gravel rose from 6.5% to 10% of non-electric bike sales in Germany between 2024 and 2025. Austria sold 29,000 gravel bikes in 2025, up 48%.

Search and sales

Industry sales reports point to gravel sales still growing, while the searches in this report have held broadly level since 2023/24. Both can be true. Search measures how much people research, not how many bikes they buy, and several things pull the two apart.

  • Fewer searches per buyer. In 2021 to 2023 many riders were finding out what gravel was. A familiar category needs less research before a purchase, so search per sale falls as it matures.
  • A larger share of a smaller pool. Gravel's share of bike searches has risen every year. If the bike market as a whole recovers, a rising share means more gravel sales even when gravel searches are flat.
  • Value, not volume. Higher-specification bikes and e-gravel raise what is spent per bike. Search counts interest, not spend.
  • Discounting. The industry cleared excess stock with heavy discounting from 2023. Lower prices move bikes to people already in the market without adding new searches.
  • Research beyond Google. YouTube, Strava, social media, retailer apps and AI answers now carry research that used to start on Google, and Google's own AI Overviews answer many questions on the results page. Google search volume understates total interest by a little more each year.
  • Timing. Search leads purchases by months, and this report runs September to August while sales data runs by calendar year.

Read the search data as a measure of attention and research. It is most useful for comparing brands, models, kit and events against each other, and for spotting where interest is moving.

Searches for named models

Generic terms like "gravel bike" are a weak guide to buying, because people who know what they want search for a brand or a model. We mapped the gravel and road ranges of 43 bike brands sold in the UK, from 2022 to 2026, from premium brands to value ranges such as Decathlon's Van Rysel and Triban, Carrera and Voodoo. For each brand we pulled every search term naming a gravel model, from "canyon grizl" to "giant revolt advanced 2 review", plus every "brand gravel bike" term, such as "specialized gravel bike" or "gravel bike canyon". Road works the same way, with model names plus "brand road bike" terms. That is about 4,900 search terms with at least 20 searches a month, with duplicate variants removed.

03How searches changed after 2023

Searches for every type of bike have fallen since 2023/24. Gravel searches fell in line with the rest, not faster. This looks like search interest cooling after the pandemic boom, not a loss of interest in gravel specifically.

Two things stand out inside that decline. Searches with "uk" added moved towards gravel, with "gravel bike uk" rising from 17,700 to 20,000 while "road bike uk" halved from 8,440 to 4,140. And event searches went the other way from bike searches, covered in section 06.

The biggest events are full

The biggest events have stopped growing because they are full. Unbound in Kansas runs at about 5,000 riders and is limited by local accommodation, with half its places now allocated through qualifiers. SBT GRVL in Colorado works to a permit cap of 1,800. In the UK, Dirty Reiver sold out its 2026 edition in six hours.

The Gravel Earth series fell from 25 events to 12 after organisers left, reportedly over fees, and several moved to the UCI series. That is a sign of events choosing a stronger platform, not of riders walking away.

!

Search interest in gravel bikes has found a steady level. More growth in search interest depends on reaching people who do not yet ride.

04Who the gravel rider is

There is no recent, reliable UK survey of gravel riders. We used three sources instead. Google search demographics, event data and Strava's published reports.

Gravel bike searches skew more male than road or mountain bike. Gravel racing is the reverse. Women's interest in gravel racing is close to men's, and well ahead of their interest in road racing. Gravel events, with mixed fields, social formats and women's waves, appear to be the more welcoming door.

What riders and events tell us

  • More than 40% of riders at the first Gralloch in 2023 were doing their first gravel race.
  • Strava reported gravel uploads up 55% globally in 2023, ahead of mountain biking at 13% and e-bikes at 23%.
  • Strava's UK data shows cycling among under-35s up 80% since 2019, and among women up 20%. These figures cover all cycling, not gravel alone.
  • Strava's 2025 survey found riders see gravel and mountain biking as having high barriers to entry.

The picture is a 35 to 44 core who already ride, with a younger, more female edge coming in through events. Both are well placed for brands and events that make starting easy.

05How gravel riders shop

Gravel riders search like experienced riders. Generic and model searches are now similar in size for gravel and road. Clickstream data puts "gravel bike" and "gravel bikes" at about 22,000 searches a month, against 21,400 for road. The difference is in what they search for next.

Three patterns matter for brands.

  • Gravel kit searches are holding better than road. Gravel wheel searches rose 14%, and gravel tyres and shoes held level. Over the same years road shoes and road wheels each fell 29%, and road groupsets 22%. Gravel groupsets fell 7%. Helmets were the exception, growing on both sides.
  • Bikepacking searches are cooling. Gravel and bikepacking bag searches fell by a third, from about 60,000 to 40,500 a year. Bikepacking bags remain the largest single term at almost 18,000 a year.
  • Gravel apparel is not a search category. Gravel jerseys and shorts together draw under 15,000 searches a year and are falling. Road apparel is just as small. Riders search for cycling clothing in general, so apparel brands win gravel riders through fit, use and imagery, not a gravel label.

Advice-led searches such as "beginner gravel ride uk", "gravel cycling uk" and "gravel events uk" have no clear owner. Whoever answers them well reaches new riders before they choose a bike or a shop.

06Events

Events are where gravel search interest is still growing. Across about 520 search terms with at least 20 searches a month, from "gravel race uk" and "gravel events 2026" to "the gralloch 2026", UK searches for gravel events rose from about 146,000 to 193,000 a year, up 32%. Road event searches, from sportives to criteriums, rose 15% to about 352,000. Road is still larger, but the gap has narrowed from about two to one to 1.8 to one.

Includes named events and general searches such as "gravel race uk" and "sportive near me". Spectator searches for world championships, the Olympics and the Commonwealth Games are excluded. UK searches only, so entrants from overseas are not counted.

We took the same approach to events, pulling every search term for 14 gravel and road events and removing terms that belong to something else. The UK calendar

  • The Gralloch, run by RED:ON in Galloway, grew from 1,230 riders in 2023 to more than 3,000 from 47 nations in 2026. It is the UK's UCI Gravel World Series round.
  • Dirty Reiver, run by Focal Events in Kielder, had about 2,000 riders in 2025 and sold out 2026 in six hours.
  • Glorious Gravel runs more than 30 rides a year with no cut-offs, e-bikes welcome, a women's wave and a first-ride guide. Its British Gravel Trophy adds six 50 to 60km rounds with age bands and women's standings.
  • The British Gravel Championships in 2025 were run by RED:ON at Dalby with at least three times the field of earlier editions and a free YouTube livestream. The 2026 championships, run by Outsider Events at Ae Forest, were described by road.cc as relatively small.

07The riders not here yet

The evidence on cycling and health is compelling. Cycling is associated with longer, healthier and happier lives. People who cycled to work in a study of 260,000 UK adults had 41% lower mortality, and cycling is linked to about 21% fewer days of poor mental health. Two hours a week in nature is linked to better health and wellbeing. These are associations from large studies, not guarantees, but they are consistent.

In England, 11.8 million adults, about a quarter, do less than 30 minutes of activity a week. Activity is lower among women, the least affluent, disabled people and Black and Asian adults.

Traffic is the main barrier, and gravel routes run mostly away from it, on forest roads, bridleways and byways. The constraint is access. Only about 22% of rights of way in England and Wales can legally be ridden, and route information is scattered. Forests that already welcome hundreds of millions of visits are an obvious starting point.

✓

The people gravel can reach are those who want to ride but avoid traffic, and those who want a fitness goal without the culture of road racing. Both groups are large, and neither is well served today.

08The support gap

Gravel in the UK has been built by independent organisers, retailers and brands. The governing body has come late.

  • British Cycling recognised gravel as a discipline in mid-2025. Its website, checked on 12 October 2026, lists BMX, cyclo-cross and mountain bike under off-road, with no gravel section.
  • Its strategy to 2029 does not mention gravel. UK Sport has committed £38.95 million for the LA28 cycle, mostly Olympic track, which runs a national series of six rounds.
  • Across seven UK gravel searches, British Cycling ranks first for "gravel championships uk", fourth and eighth for two racing queries, and outside the top ten for events and beginner rides. It draws an estimated 24 visits a month from gravel searches.
  • Membership fell from 137,968 to 117,475 in two years, and grassroots road racing continues to shrink, with 23 events cancelled in 2025.

This is a gap the rest of the industry can fill, and an opening for the governing body to back the discipline with the widest appeal to new riders.

09What it means for you

The common thread is making the first ride easy. Each part of the industry has a different part to play.

Bike brands

Lead with the model, sell the first ride

Riders search by model name, and the top five gravel models take more than a third of gravel model search. Model pages, reviews and comparisons carry the weight. Cube and Trek show that a gravel model can double its search share in three seasons, and Cannondale shows how fast it can fall away. Pair entry models with route guides and event partnerships.

Component and kit brands

Own the gravel-specific categories

Searches for gravel wheels, tyres and shoes held or grew over three years while searches for their road equivalents fell by up to 29%. Make the gravel use obvious in product names and content, and explain the choices to new riders who do not yet know what a flared bar or a 1x drivetrain is for.

Apparel brands

Sell the ride, not the label

Hardly anyone searches for gravel clothing. Riders search for cycling clothing and choose on fit and use. Show kit on mixed-ability gravel rides, in women's and larger sizes, and through events that already attract the newer audience.

Event organisers

Grow the entry level, not just the start line

The flagship events are full. Search interest is growing fastest for organisers with many dates, short distances, no cut-offs and women's waves, the model Glorious Gravel has proved. Partner with forests and brands to add capacity close to where people live.

Retailers

Answer the beginner questions

Retailers already win most gravel search traffic. The beginner searches, from "beginner gravel ride uk" to "gravel events uk", have no clear owner. Shop rides, local route guides and event entry bundles turn that traffic into new customers.

Governing bodies and land managers

Make gravel easy to find and legal to ride

Give gravel a home on the website, a place in the strategy and a domestic series that feeds the UCI pathway. Land managers and access bodies can open and signpost forest roads and byways, the routes that take new riders away from traffic.

i

Better data would help everyone. Brand-level sales, event entries and participation data are held separately. Pooled, anonymised data would give the industry a clearer view of who is starting gravel and where they stop.

10Method and sources

Search data covers Google UK, September 2022 to August 2026, grouped into four 12-month years. All figures are absolute search counts. We avoided percentage changes on small numbers, where a rise from 1 to 2 shows as 100%. Market figures are from public reporting only.

Method notes and limitations
  • Search volumes are Google Ads estimates via DataForSEO. Google groups close variants, so terms such as "gravel tyres" and "gravel tires" are counted once.
  • Bike brands and models. We mapped the gravel and road ranges of 43 brands for 2022 to 2026, using brand sites and launch coverage, then pulled every UK search term containing the brand and model name from DataForSEO Labs, plus every term combining the brand with "gravel bike" or "road bike", more than 22,000 terms in all. Terms naming a model are counted under the model, not twice. Historical volumes come from Google Ads. Variants that Google groups together, such as word order and plurals, are counted once.
  • Coverage. Value and direct brands are included: Decathlon (Van Rysel and Triban, counted as one), Carrera, Voodoo, Planet X, Boardman, Mason, Vielo and Dolan. Halfords is a retailer, so its own "halfords gravel bike" searches are not counted. Voodoo Nakisi is counted as gravel, as Halfords sells it as an adventure gravel bike.
  • Kit. Every term naming a kit category as gravel or road was pulled from DataForSEO Labs, about 7,000 in all, with street names, other products and motorbike terms removed. Groupsets compare Shimano GRX, SRAM XPLR and Campagnolo Ekar with Shimano 105, Ultegra and Dura-Ace.
  • Minimum volume. Only search terms with at least 20 UK searches a month, in at least one of the four years, are counted. Below that, Google's estimates are least precise, and the search-term tool also stops listing them for the largest models. The findings were tested with no minimum and with minimums of 20, 30 and 50 a month. Gravel's share rises by about two points at every level, and the top four brands stay the same.
  • Spelling variants. Model names were checked with and without hyphens and spaces, such as "ultra-grit" and "ultra grit", "all-grit" and "allgrit", "top stone" and "topstone". Google reports some variants as one search and some separately. Separate ones are added, and grouped ones are counted once.
  • Classification. Time trial, cyclocross and e-road models are excluded. All-road models count as road. Cannondale SuperX and Trek Boone are treated as cyclocross. Comparison terms naming two brands are excluded. Model names that are also places, such as Marin Headlands, are excluded.
  • Validation. Brand shares of gravel model search from Google Ads were checked against DataForSEO clickstream data for the latest 12 months. Most brands fall within one percentage point. Specialized is the largest gap, at 12.7% on Google Ads and 8.9% on clickstream.
  • Event totals combine searches for 14 named events with general event searches such as "gravel race uk", "gravel ride near me", "sportive events uk" and "criterium race", about 1,500 terms in all, with each term counted once. Spectator searches for world championships and the Olympics, and unrelated uses of "sportive", are removed.
  • Events. Every search term for each event was pulled, and terms belonging to something else with the same name were removed, including the Battle on the Beach TV series, deer gralloching, Traka key systems and the Legoland Dragon ride. Haute Route is excluded because cycling interest cannot be separated from the hiking and ski routes. RideLondon and Raid Pyrenees are not included.
  • Gender and age data is Google Trends interest among searchers, not a count of riders.
  • SERP traffic is an estimate from DataForSEO Labs based on rankings and search volume.
  • Sales figures come from public reports of Bicycle Association, ZIV and Austrian industry data. No confidential data is published here.
