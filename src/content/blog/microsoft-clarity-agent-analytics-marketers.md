AI agents that shop, book and fill in forms for people now get their own report in Microsoft Clarity, the free analytics tool, after an update on 4 November 2026. Clarity's own data recorded checkout in 25% of sessions involving an agent, so ecommerce and lead generation teams can now see where agents finish a job and where they stall.

## Key takeaways

- Microsoft added an Agent Activity dashboard and agent simulation assessments to Microsoft Clarity on 4 November 2026, and says they are available globally to all Clarity users.
- Microsoft says weekly sessions from AI agents nearly doubled in three weeks to almost 2 million in its own Clarity data.
- In 5.23 million agentic sessions between 13 August and 9 September 2026, Clarity recorded checkout in 25.21% of sessions, against 14.65% of human sessions on the same sites.
- Clarity currently detects agents from three operators, Google, OpenAI and Anthropic.
- Eligible Shopify and WooCommerce merchants using Clarity Brand Agents can now have products appear in Microsoft Copilot, Bing and MSN.

## What Microsoft added to Clarity

Nabil Naghdy announced three changes on the [Clarity blog](https://clarity.microsoft.com/blog/clarity-connects-agents-LLMs-people/). The first is Agentic Analytics. Its Agent Activity dashboard shows how agent traffic moves over time, which agents arrive and what they do once they land, from searches, logins and downloads to form submissions, appointment bookings and checkout. Simulation assessments then send an agent through a task on your site and report whether it finished, how long it took, how many interactions it needed and where it struggled. Microsoft is starting with commerce and travel scenarios.

The second is an expansion of Brand Agents, Clarity's on-site shopping assistant. Products from eligible WooCommerce and Shopify merchants can now appear across Copilot, Bing and MSN, and Microsoft's [Brand Agents documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/brand-agents/brand-agents-overview) says accepting the terms publishes the catalogue to Microsoft Merchant Center. Brand Agents also now serves local businesses on WordPress, booking appointments through tools such as Square, Calendly, HubSpot and Salesforce.

The third is Ask Clarity, which answers plain-language questions about site behaviour and links its findings to session recordings. It is in closed beta for retailers.

## What do AI agents do on a website?

Microsoft published the numbers behind the launch in an [earlier Clarity study](https://clarity.microsoft.com/blog/agentic-analytics-what-agents-do-on-websites/) of 5.23 million agentic sessions across 230,511 Clarity projects. It compared how often each action appeared in sessions involving an agent and in human sessions on the same sites.

| Action recorded | Share of agentic sessions | Share of human sessions |
|---|---|---|
| Checkout | 25.21% | 14.65% |
| Submit form | 19.85% | 18.56% |
| Outbound click | 14.28% | 26.99% |
| Search | 7.16% | 4.79% |

Checkout appeared in more than 306,621 agentic sessions, form submissions in more than 241,391 and appointment bookings in more than 102,401. Humans click away to other sites far more often, while agents arrive with a task and head for the form or the basket. Microsoft adds a caveat of its own, because a session can include a person taking over from the agent, and Clarity can't yet split the two.

## Where agents hit your funnel

For an ecommerce or lead generation team, agents now behave like a customer segment with its own conversion path, and until this week most teams had no report that separated them. Agents reach the two pages where a website makes money, the checkout and the enquiry form, and those pages often carry the pop-ups, address finders, CAPTCHAs and multi-step validation that trip up software.

Our view at Manual Focus is that every brand selling or taking bookings online should run an agent simulation on its checkout and its main enquiry form before Black Friday, because an agent that fails at checkout leaves no complaint and no abandoned basket email, only a lost sale that never shows up as one. Adobe forecast a 130% rise in AI referrals to US retail sites this season, which we covered in [our Black Friday post](/blog/adobe-ai-shopping-forecast-black-friday/), and the agents behind them are the always-on kind OpenAI showed with [dots](/blog/openai-dots-chatgpt-plugins-marketing-teams/) and Anthropic with [Claude Duet](/blog/claude-duet-proactive-agent-marketing-teams/).

The update also changes who owns the problem. Agent readiness has sat with SEO teams as a crawling question, but a failed agent checkout belongs to the ecommerce manager and the developer who owns the form. Put it in the conversion rate optimisation backlog, alongside the human fixes.

## Should you publish your catalogue to Copilot?

Brand Agents gives Shopify and WooCommerce merchants a short route into Copilot and Bing shopping answers, and the decision is commercial as much as technical.

| Your situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Shopify or WooCommerce, products you want in AI answers | Test Brand Agents and accept catalogue publishing to Microsoft Merchant Center |
| Shopify or WooCommerce, strict data or privacy policy | Read the Brand Agents terms first, since conversation data is shared with Microsoft |
| Another platform | Use Agent Activity for measurement now, as Brand Agents covers only Shopify and WooCommerce |
| Local service business on WordPress | Try the booking agent with your existing CRM and check the leads it books |

Smaller brands have the most to gain, since a [Lightspeed study](/blog/ai-shopping-assistants-favour-big-retailers/) found AI assistants picked national chains over smaller rivals up to 94% of the time. Merchants who switch Brand Agents on must also add Microsoft's disclosure wording to their privacy statement.

## Five checks before Black Friday

1. **Turn on Agent Activity.** If Clarity is already on your site, open the new dashboard and note which agents visit and which actions they reach. If it isn't, install it on your main domain this week so you have a baseline before peak trading.
2. **Run a simulation on checkout.** Test guest checkout, a discount code and delivery selection, then fix the step where the agent stalls. Repeat for your highest-value enquiry or booking form.
3. **Add agent tests to your site audit.** Fold simulation results into the regular review in our [site audit and refresh playbook](/lens/ops/site-audit-and-refresh/), so a form change doesn't quietly break agent journeys after launch.
4. **Report agents as their own segment.** Keep agent sessions out of human conversion rates, or a rise in agent visits will distort the numbers you report upward. The [attribution tear-down playbook](/lens/demand/attribution-teardown/) shows how to split channels cleanly.
5. **Decide on Brand Agents.** If you run Shopify or WooCommerce, decide with legal whether to publish your catalogue to Microsoft Merchant Center, using the grid above.

## What Clarity doesn't tell you

Microsoft hasn't explained how Clarity identifies agents, and only Google, OpenAI and Anthropic operators are supported, so agents from other companies may go uncounted. Sessions that mix an agent with a human handoff are counted together, so the checkout figures describe sessions involving agents rather than purchases an agent completed alone. Simulations cover commerce and travel scenarios only for now. The study ran from 13 August to 9 September 2026 and describes sites that already receive agent traffic, so a typical site may see much less. Microsoft says the new capabilities are available globally, but it hasn't defined which merchants are eligible for Copilot product discovery or confirmed when Ask Clarity leaves beta.
