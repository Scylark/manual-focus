Microsoft Advertising confirmed on 19 November 2026 that ads now appear inside Bing's AI Overviews in every market except China, the UK included, with no new campaign needed. Existing Performance Max and AI Max campaigns serve there already, so UK paid teams on Microsoft Advertising are buying AI answer placements this Black Friday whether they planned to or not.

## Key takeaways

- Microsoft Advertising said on 19 November 2026 that ads in AI Overviews on Bing are available in all markets except China.
- The ads run through existing campaigns, including Performance Max and AI Max, and need no separate set-up.
- Search Engine Roundtable first reported sponsored results inside Bing AI Overviews on 30 October 2026, three weeks before Microsoft confirmed them.
- Microsoft's announcement doesn't mention a separate report, label or opt-out for the AI Overview placement.
- In the same update Microsoft said its new bidding models give Maximize Conversion Value and Target ROAS advertisers 10% more conversion value per click on average at a similar ROAS.

## What Microsoft confirmed on 19 November

The confirmation sits in Microsoft Advertising's [November product update](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/november-2026/put-ai-to-work-engaging-customers-in-more-places-whats-new-in-microsoft-advertising-this-november). Microsoft calls ads in AI Overviews "the latest step in bringing ads to AI experiences, building on ads in Copilot." Ads show beside an AI-generated answer when the advertiser's offer is relevant, and Microsoft's example is a hotel ad sitting next to an AI itinerary for a trip to Snowdonia National Park, a UK example in a global post.

Microsoft says "There's no separate campaign set-up required" and that the ads "run through the campaigns you already use, including Performance Max and AI Max." It backs the move with one number, a 21% increase in purchases after a Copilot interaction, from its own data on global Edge users between 21 and 31 December 2024. That figure is about Copilot, not AI Overviews, and it is almost two years old.

The confirmation came late. Khushal Bherwani posted a screenshot of a sponsored result inside a Bing AI Overview, which [Search Engine Roundtable reported on 30 October](https://www.seroundtable.com/bing-ads-copilot-ai-overviews-42416.html). Barry Schwartz [covered Microsoft's confirmation](https://www.seroundtable.com/microsoft-ads-in-ai-overviews-on-bing-42578.html) on 19 November, so some advertisers' ads may have been serving in AI answers for about three weeks before anyone told them.

The same update carried three changes that affect how those ads get built. AI Max URL inclusions let advertisers name the pages AI Max may use for landing pages, ad text and search term matching, and they are rolling out now. AI content transparency controls, now generally available, let advertisers declare AI-generated images and video and add a watermark where the format supports one. Microsoft also says its automated bidding has had its biggest upgrade in a decade, adjusting nearly twice as fast when a target changes.

## How does Bing compare with Google?

Microsoft has reached the UK first. Google's [help page on ads in AI-powered experiences](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097) lists the United States and eleven other markets for ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode, and the UK isn't among them. Google [said on 28 October](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/) that it plans to expand to more locales in the fourth quarter of 2026, "including Brazil, Mexico, and the United Kingdom", with no date.

| Question | Microsoft Advertising, Bing AI Overviews | Google Ads, AI Overviews and AI Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Live in the UK? | Yes, all markets except China from 19 November 2026 | Not listed yet, UK due in Q4 2026 |
| Campaigns that serve | Existing campaigns, including Performance Max and AI Max | Search, Shopping and Performance Max, automatically |
| New set-up needed? | No | No |
| Separate opt-out? | Not mentioned in the announcement | No, can't opt out separately from Search |
| Separate reporting? | Not mentioned in the announcement | No, included in aggregate in standard reports |

Both platforms have made the same design choice, folding AI answer placements into the campaigns you already run and leaving the advertiser to work out what changed. We set out Google's version in [what ads in Google's AI Mode mean for UK paid search teams](/blog/google-ai-mode-ads-uk-paid-search/), and most of that advice now applies to Microsoft a few weeks earlier.

## What changes for paid and SEO teams?

Paid search teams carry most of it. In many brands the Microsoft Advertising budget is the smaller one and gets less scrutiny, and some accounts are imported from Google Ads and left to run. Those accounts now serve into a new placement during peak trading, and any shift in cost per click or conversion rate over Black Friday will mix seasonality with the new inventory. Teams running AI Max will see the widest change, because AI Max decides which queries to match and what the ad says, and people ask AI answers in full questions rather than the short keywords most accounts were built around.

SEO and content teams are affected too. A sponsored result next to the AI answer competes with the sources Bing cites in it. Bing Webmaster Tools already shows which grounding queries cite your pages, as we covered in [what Bing's Topics Portfolio means for AI search content plans](/blog/bing-topics-portfolio-ai-citation-gaps/), so the two teams can now look at the same queries from both sides.

Our view at Manual Focus is that UK advertisers should treat Bing's AI Overview ads as a live test of the Google launch still to come, because the mechanics match, Microsoft got there first and a smaller budget makes mistakes cheaper to find. Whatever you learn about query mix, landing pages and creative control on Bing in the next six weeks will be useful when Google switches on the UK. The pattern is familiar from [ChatGPT Ads turning on creative rewrites by default](/blog/chatgpt-ads-creative-enhancements-default/), where the platform made the change and the advertiser found the controls afterwards.

## What to do before Black Friday

1. **Check what is live.** List every Microsoft Advertising campaign running in the UK, and flag the Performance Max and AI Max ones, since Microsoft names them as eligible. Note which were imported from Google Ads and haven't been reviewed since.
2. **Set a baseline today.** Export the last eight weeks of Microsoft Advertising search terms, cost per click and conversion rate by campaign, so a change after 19 November is visible against something.
3. **Add URL inclusions to AI Max.** Point AI Max at the pages you want it to use, such as gift guides and best-selling product pages, and keep it away from thin or out-of-date pages that could end up in ad text.
4. **Tighten negatives.** Add negative keywords for informational and comparison phrasing that has never converted, because question-style searches are likely to pull those in. The [paid-search bidding agent playbook](/lens/demand/paid-search-bidding-agent/) covers margin-aware checks that suit this review.
5. **Plan a January holdout.** If Microsoft doesn't add an AI Overview segment, a geo or time-based holdout is the only way to judge what the placement adds. Our [attribution tear-down playbook](/lens/demand/attribution-teardown/) shows how to set one up.

## What Microsoft hasn't said

The announcement gives no reporting detail, so nobody outside Microsoft knows whether AI Overview impressions will get their own segment or stay blended with Search. It doesn't mention an opt-out or say how the ads are labelled. Microsoft published no click-through or conversion data for the AI Overview placement itself, and the one purchase figure it gives is from Copilot users in late 2024. It also doesn't say whether Shopping or standard Search campaigns serve there in the same way as Performance Max and AI Max, or how often Bing shows an AI Overview at all in the UK. Anyone planning budget around the placement is working without the numbers that would size it.
